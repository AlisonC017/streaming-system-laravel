About the show

When a rolling blackout drops the coastal city of Marrow Bay into darkness, Detective Ø Halloran is the only officer who saw the last flicker of the grid — and the only one who can't trust his own memory to describe it. Diagnosed with early anterograde amnesia, he has one long night, a pocket recorder, and a boulevard full of witnesses who each remember a different version of the truth.

Across four seasons, Nightfall Boulevard peels back a single city block at a time — a shuttered cinema, a jazz bar that never closes, a rooftop that shouldn't be reachable — until the smallest lie turns out to be the one that keeps the lights off. Equal parts memory puzzle and mood piece, it's the show critics called "a city painted entirely in the blue hour."
