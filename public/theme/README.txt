Streamline — free cinematic streaming (OTT) website template by uiCookies.

Concept: a dark UI that treats "night" as a real colour rather than pure black plus one
neon pop. The whole interface sits on a deep midnight-navy ink with a subtle blue/rose
hue bias, staged like a cinema at dusk. A warm marquee-gold is the primary accent (the
theatre-lights glow used on CTAs, ranks and prices) and a spotlight-rose is the sparing
secondary (ratings, "New" flags, sale badges). Two full pages ship: a long-scroll landing
(hero, Top-10 scroll-snap row, genre tiles, spotlight split, devices band, 3-tier pricing
with a monthly/annual toggle, testimonials, FAQ) and a title-detail page (backdrop hero,
synopsis, cast row, facts sidebar, 4-season episode switcher).

Palette:
  #0b0e18  midnight ink (base)
  #161b2b  surface / cards
  #2b3450  hairline / borders
  #f3c15a  marquee gold (primary accent)
  #ea6079  spotlight rose (secondary accent)
  #eceef5  cool off-white (text)

Font pairing:
  Display — Bricolage Grotesque (600/700/800), for headings and the wordmark
  Body    — Hanken Grotesk (400/500/600/700), for UI and long-form copy

Tech: Bootstrap 5 (self-hosted), vanilla JS only (no jQuery), inline SVG icons only
(no icon fonts), Google Fonts, real local images in /img. Accessible (skip link, landmarks,
focus-visible, alt text, aria labels) and reduced-motion aware. © 2026.
